Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how RNA Forge handles information received through its protected website enquiry service.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Who we are

RNA Forge Ltd is responsible for personal information provided through enquiries about its services, technology, funding or collaboration activities.

Information we receive

The protected enquiry form may collect your name, work email address, organisation, enquiry type and message. Limited technical information, including network and browser signals, may be processed for abuse prevention and service security.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond, assess the requested service or collaboration, prepare quotations, manage follow-up discussions, prevent abuse and meet applicable business or legal requirements.

Service providers

The website uses Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection and a restricted server-side service to validate submissions and deliver them to RNA Forge. The website does not use advertising trackers or sell enquiry information.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers or professional advisers where reasonably necessary to operate the business, respond to the enquiry or meet legal obligations. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Your choices

You may ask RNA Forge to provide, correct or delete personal information it holds about you where applicable. You may also ask us to stop using your information for follow-up communications.

Confidential information

Do not send confidential RNA sequences, proprietary formulations, unpublished process details or sensitive commercial information through an initial public enquiry. Contact RNA Forge first so an appropriate confidential discussion can be arranged.

Contact

Use the protected contact form for privacy questions or requests.
