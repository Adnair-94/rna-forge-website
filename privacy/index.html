Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how RNA Forge handles personal information received through this static website and direct email enquiries.

Last updated: 14 August 2026.

Who we are

RNA Forge Ltd is responsible for personal information provided directly through enquiries about its services, technology, funding or collaboration activities. Privacy questions can be sent to info@rnaforge.com.

Information we receive

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, user accounts or backend form handling. If you contact RNA Forge by email, we may receive your name, contact details, organisation and the information included in your enquiry.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond, assess the requested service or collaboration, prepare quotations, manage follow-up discussions and meet applicable business or legal requirements.

Sharing and retention

RNA Forge does not sell enquiry information. Information may be shared with service providers or professional advisers where reasonably necessary to operate the business, respond to the enquiry or meet legal obligations. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Your choices

You may ask RNA Forge to provide, correct or delete personal information it holds about you where applicable. You may also ask us to stop using your information for follow-up communications.

Confidential information

Do not send confidential RNA sequences, proprietary formulations, unpublished process details or sensitive commercial information through an initial public enquiry. Contact RNA Forge first so an appropriate confidential discussion can be arranged.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@rnaforge.com.
