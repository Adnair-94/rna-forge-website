RNAbox technology

RNAbox™ is a modular, digitally enabled manufacturing platform and technology programme designed to support flexible, scalable RNA medicine manufacturing.

Context

Addressing fragmented RNA manufacturing workflows.

Current RNA manufacturing workflows are often fragmented, infrastructure-intensive and heavily reliant on offline analytics, creating major challenges around scalability, cost and manufacturing flexibility.

RNAbox is designed to address these issues through a modular manufacturing approach that supports progressive integration into existing workflows, rather than requiring complete replacement of manufacturing infrastructure.

The platform combines synthesis, purification and formulation with increasing levels of automation, real-time monitoring and data-driven process control, with the aim of improving scalability and reducing manufacturing burden.

Through supported innovation programmes, RNA Forge has advanced integrated prototype workflows and modular deployment concepts. The next stage is focused on refinement and pilot deployment planning.

Ultimately, the goal is to help enable more scalable, flexible and accessible manufacturing infrastructure for emerging RNA medicines.
