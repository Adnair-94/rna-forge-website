RNAbox technology

RNAbox™ is RNA Forge's modular manufacturing technology programme, designed to support progressive integration into existing workflows rather than requiring complete replacement of manufacturing infrastructure.

Context

More integrated RNA manufacturing workflows.

RNA manufacturing workflows are often fragmented, infrastructure-intensive and heavily reliant on offline analytics. This creates challenges around scalability, cost, flexibility and process understanding as RNA technologies expand beyond vaccines into broader therapeutic applications.

The RNAbox development roadmap combines RNA synthesis, purification and formulation modules with increasing levels of automation, monitoring and data-driven process control.

The aim is to create more flexible and integrated manufacturing workflows that reduce operational burden, improve product and process understanding, and support more scalable RNA production.

Supported innovation programmes have advanced integrated prototype workflows and modular deployment concepts. The next stage is focused on refinement, translation planning and pilot deployment preparation.
