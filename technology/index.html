RNAbox™ overview

A flexible platform vision for scalable RNA manufacturing.

RNAbox™ is RNA Forge's vision for a flexible, scalable manufacturing platform that connects unit operations, analytics and model-based monitoring and control. This enables end-to-end process visibility, precise control, and operational flexibility.

Built from real-world data: RNAbox™ is engineered directly from insights gained through RNA Forge's day-to-day custom manufacturing and bioanalytical services.

Modular manufacturing

Compact hardware designed around existing workflows.

RNAbox™ is being developed around compact, reconfigurable hardware modules and standardised interfaces to support a reduced deployment footprint and easier integration.

The modular architecture adapts to diverse process requirements, delivering consistent performance whether operating as standalone modules or fully integrated workflows.

Modular system concepts

Modular hardware for connected workflows.

Building on this modular approach, the concept illustrations show how manufacturing, fluid handling, chromatography and reactor modules could be configured within the RNAbox™ platform.

Integrated analytics & control

Connecting manufacturing with process insight.

The technology programme connects synthesis, purification and formulation with increasing levels of monitoring, analytics and model-based process monitoring and control.

The objective is to support the development and manufacturing of RNA vaccines and therapeutics, improving access to RNA technology and enabling consistent candidate production throughout the product development cycle.

Development roadmap

Platform refinement and GMP readiness.

Ongoing platform development focuses on rigorous process evaluation and aligning modular concepts with GMP standards.

Partner-led evaluations of representative workflows will assess performance, validate analytical controls, and inform early pilot deployment.
