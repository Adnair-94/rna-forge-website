RNAbox overview

A modular platform designed for progressive integration.

RNAbox is RNA Forge's manufacturing technology programme for flexible RNA medicine production. It is being developed around practical workflow constraints identified through manufacturing, analytics and technical development activities.

The programme addresses fragmented and infrastructure-intensive workflows through modular components that can be introduced progressively.

Modular manufacturing

Designed to complement existing infrastructure.

RNAbox is intended to support progressive deployment into existing workflows rather than require complete replacement of current manufacturing infrastructure.

This approach allows individual capabilities or increasing levels of integration to be evaluated as programme needs and deployment readiness develop.

Integrated analytics & control

Connecting manufacturing with process insight.

The technology programme connects synthesis, purification and formulation with increasing levels of monitoring, analytics and data-informed process control.

The objective is to improve product understanding and support more consistent decisions across RNA process development.

Development roadmap

Refinement and pilot deployment planning.

RNA Forge has advanced integrated prototype workflows and modular deployment concepts through supported innovation activities.

Current priorities are system refinement, pilot deployment preparation and future evaluation with appropriate partners and real workflow data.
