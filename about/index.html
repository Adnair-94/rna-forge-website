About

Built at the interface of RNA manufacturing, bioanalytics and process engineering.

Company

A University of Sheffield spinout.

RNA Forge is a University of Sheffield spinout operating at the interface of RNA manufacturing, bioanalytics and process engineering.

We work across the RNA development ecosystem, engaging with academic groups, biotech companies, pharma partners, CDMOs, CROs, technology providers and funders. Our aim is to complement existing manufacturing infrastructure rather than replace it outright.

Today, RNA Forge delivers specialised RNA manufacturing and analytical services. In parallel, these activities inform the development of RNAbox™, our modular manufacturing technology programme for more integrated, scalable and digitally enabled RNA production.

Approach

Closing the loop between science and engineering.

Our approach is to close the loop between scientific expertise and engineering: using practical manufacturing and analytical insight to guide technologies that can reduce development timelines, lower manufacturing burden and improve product understanding.

Team

Expertise across RNA bioprocess engineering, analytics and translation.

RNA Forge brings together expertise across RNA bioprocess engineering, advanced analytics, biotech operations and commercial translation. The team combines academic innovation with industrial and entrepreneurial experience across RNA manufacturing, analytical science, process development and company building.
