About

Combining RNA manufacturing, advanced analytics and process innovation.

RNA Forge is a University of Sheffield spinout founded in response to manufacturing challenges in RNA therapeutics.

Company

Operating at the centre of the RNA development ecosystem.

RNA Forge is a University of Sheffield spinout operating at the centre of the RNA development ecosystem, engaging across academia, biotech, pharma and CDMOs/CROs.

RNA Forge is positioned to collaborate with and complement existing infrastructure, including technology and equipment providers. This enables both near-term service delivery and longer-term platform deployment across multiple stakeholders.

RNA Forge operates across development, manufacturing and analytics through service delivery today. Service activities generate practical data and workflow insight that directly inform RNAbox™ development.

Approach

Closing the loop between scientific expertise and engineering.

Our approach is to close the loop between scientific expertise and engineering. We run specialised RNA manufacturing and analytical services for biotech and academic clients, and use those real-world insights to design next-generation engineering solutions such as RNAbox™.

Ecosystem

Collaborating with and complementing existing infrastructure.

RNA Forge works with the wider RNA development ecosystem, including academic groups, biotech companies, pharma partners, CDMOs, CROs, technology providers and funders.

The company is designed to support near-term RNA manufacturing and analytical service needs while building modular platform technologies that can be progressively deployed across existing workflows.
