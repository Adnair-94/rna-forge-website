Development roadmap

RNAbox™ development roadmap.

From integrated prototype development to strategic partner programmes, with evidence and deployment requirements reviewed at each stage.

  1. 1
    2023–2026Prove

    Integrated prototype development

    Developing connected RNA synthesis, purification and formulation workflows within an integrated platform programme.

  2. 2
    2024–2027Optimise

    Advanced RNAbox™ technologies

    Advancing process optimisation, analytics, soft-sensor development, recycling concepts and digital control.

  3. 3
    2027Evaluate

    Next-generation platform evaluation

    Building evidence through representative integrated workflows, comparability planning and stability studies.

  4. 4
    2027–2028Prepare

    Quality and deployment preparation

    Developing design controls, single-use component strategy, supply-chain planning and deployment documentation.

  5. 5
    2028–2029Partner

    Strategic partner programmes

    Preparing partner-specific engineering, technology transfer and evaluation plans for defined deployment programmes.
