Batch documentation

Certificate of Analysis supplied with catalogue batches.

Samples are supplied with a Certificate of Analysis generated from comprehensive characterisation of the production batch.

  • RNA content quantification by ultraviolet (UV) spectroscopy
  • Size and integrity quantification by Fragment Analyzer
  • Residual nucleoside triphosphate (NTP) quantification by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC)
  • Sequence identity confirmation by high-resolution liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS)
  • Poly(A) tail length and heterogeneity quantification by high-resolution LC-MS
  • 5′ capping efficiency quantification by high-resolution LC-MS
