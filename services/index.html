Services

RNA manufacturing and analytical services

RNA Forge provides off-the-shelf mRNAs, custom manufacturing, analytical services and technical development support for research and translational RNA programmes.

Service-informed development

Generate material, analytical insight and workflow knowledge.

Our manufacturing and analytical service activities help teams generate RNA material, understand product quality and assess process performance.

These activities also inform RNAbox development by keeping platform priorities connected to real manufacturing bottlenecks, customer needs and workflow constraints.

Off-the-shelf mRNAs

Predefined reporter mRNA materials for assay development, method setup, screening and non-clinical research applications.

Custom manufacturing

Custom RNA production and process development support for research and translational applications, quoted according to construct, amount, format and analytical requirements.

Analytical services

Advanced analytical workflows providing deeper insight into RNA quality, integrity and process performance.

Technical development support

Focused technical input on RNA manufacturing workflows, analytical planning and manufacturability considerations.
