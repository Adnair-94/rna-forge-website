RNA Forge is a University of Sheffield spinout developing modular RNA manufacturing technologies and providing RNA manufacturing and bioanalytical services for RNA therapeutic developers.

Explore servicesSee RNAbox technology

What we do

RNA manufacturing built around earlier product understanding.

RNA therapeutics are expanding beyond vaccines into areas including oncology, rare disease and gene editing. That growth is creating demand for manufacturing technologies that are flexible, scalable, analytically informed and easier to integrate into evolving workflows.

RNA Forge was founded to address these challenges. We are developing RNAbox™, a modular RNA manufacturing technology programme designed to support progressive integration into existing manufacturing workflows, while delivering specialised RNA manufacturing and analytical services today.
